# Contributing to MÉTIS

MÉTIS is a research archive. Contributions should include a precise research question, controlled baselines, fixed models and random seeds, raw observations, reproducible analysis, uncertainty and limitations.

Preserve negative findings and failed replications. Do not describe archived P2 material as independently verified P3 evidence. Do not add unrelated private projects, identifying host-local paths, credentials or runtime caches.

When redacting a historical text artifact for privacy, retain its original SHA-256 in the provenance register, record the curated digest and explain the transformation. Do not silently alter experimental outcomes.

See [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) and [RELEASE_GATE.md](RELEASE_GATE.md).
